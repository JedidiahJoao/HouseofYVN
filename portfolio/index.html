Client 01 · Style strategy

Building a Wardrobe
Beyond the Essentials

7 months · 3 shopping phases · 3 markets

She came to House of YVN in search of a wardrobe that could better support her professional lifestyle, beginning with a need for versatile workwear. What followed was a deeper exploration of her personal style, wardrobe needs and the image she wanted to project.

Through a discovery consultation, wardrobe analysis and three phased shopping experiences, we curated a wardrobe sourced across Kenyan, UK and US retailers — bringing together intentional silhouettes, quality pieces and versatility.

Client 02 · Event styling

A Garden Affair,
Styled with Intention.

She approached House of YVN in October seeking the perfect look for a garden wedding. We explored local boutiques and contemporary African designers to curate an ensemble that balanced elegance, comfort and personality.

From colour exploration and chic jewellery to heels designed for comfort through the ceremony, reception and after-party, every detail was considered to complement her refined, classy aesthetic — without compromising on ease of movement.

Client 03 · Bespoke graduation styling

From a Conversation to a
Graduation Editorial.

4 weeks · 2 looks · Bespoke styling

What began as a casual conversation to pick a stylist's brain evolved into a four-week styling project for her graduation shoot. Together, we developed two looks from concept to execution, beginning with a discovery consultation, design conceptualisation and conversations around body architecture, colour combinations and silhouette.

From the tailor's first fitting to final adjustments, each look was refined with intention. On shoot day, we curated footwear and accessories, offered guidance on posing and hair, and made on-set garment adjustments to ensure every frame captured the vision seamlessly.
