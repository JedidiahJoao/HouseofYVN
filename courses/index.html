Bespoke corporate programmes

For groups exceeding 30 participants, extended sessions or specialised organisational needs, custom packages are available upon consultation.

    The Style Conversation

    An engaging introduction to personal style, identity, confidence and professional presence.

    Understanding Your Style

    A practical session exploring personal preferences, colour, fit, silhouette and dressing for different lifestyles and professional environments.

  3. 03

    Practical lessons on building versatile wardrobes, coordinating outfits, shopping intentionally and getting more out of existing clothes.
