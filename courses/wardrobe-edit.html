Knowing What You Own

Learn to look at your wardrobe with a more intentional eye. Understand what works, what does not, what is missing and what deserves to stay.

This course turns your wardrobe from a collection of purchases into a functional, expressive system—one that supports your real life and makes getting dressed easier.

Knowledge for
your wardrobe.

  • Identifying wardrobe gaps
  • What to keep, alter, style or let go

  • Building a functional wardrobe
